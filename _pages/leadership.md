  <p class="lead">Faculty In-Charge of AIT's Open Source Software (OSS) Club and its national hackathon, Innerve.</p>
  <div class="stat-strip">
    <div class="stat"><span class="stat-number">9,500+</span><span class="stat-label">students, Innerve 9.0</span></div>
    <div class="stat"><span class="stat-number">2,500+</span><span class="stat-label">teams, Innerve 9.0</span></div>

  <p>AIT's clubs page, which covers 2018–19 to 2022–23, lists her as the club's staff in-charge. She is currently Faculty In-Charge, with Prof. Kuldeep Hule as co-in-charge. The club was founded in 2014; its motto is <em>"May The Source Be With You!"</em></p>
  <p>In addition to Innerve, the club runs Anubhav (interview experiences), front-end classes and GRAPHICA, a hackathon for first-year students.</p>
  <p>Innerve, "Nerve to Innovate", has been held since 2016, with free registration for participants.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Innerve 7, 2023.</strong> Judge.</li>
    <li><strong>Innerve 9.0, 7–8 February 2025.</strong> 31 finalist teams, a 24-hour finale and 10 technology verticals. Guided by Prof. Ingale and Prof. Kuldeep Hule as faculty in-charge.</li>
    <li><strong>Innerve X, finale 30–31 January 2026.</strong> 10,000+ registrations, 45 finalist teams, Major League Hacking (MLH) as event partner. Held under the guidance of the OSS Club faculty.</li>
  </ul>
  <p class="card-source">Sources: Punekar News, February 2025; Free Press Journal and Sahyadri Startups, January 2026; Innerve on Devpost and Devfolio.</p>
</section>
