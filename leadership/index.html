September 2026 · Conference Chair

ICNDIA-2026

The first IEEE international conference held at AIT. She was one of its two Conference Chairs.

1,500+submissions
237accepted
217presented
6parallel tracks

The full name is the IEEE International Conference on Nexus of Digitalization, Intelligence and Applications. Her own Department of Information Technology organised it on 18–19 September 2026, on campus and online. She shared the chair with Dr. Ashwini Sapkal.

Every paper went through double-blind review. Accepted papers go to IEEE Xplore, and the proceedings were released on the first day.

Lt Gen Vipul Shinghal, AVSM, SM, Deputy Chief of Army Staff (Information Systems & Technology), was chief guest at the opening. Keynotes came from Prof. Mukul S. Sutaone (Director, IIIT Allahabad), Lt Gen (Dr) S. P. Kochhar (Retd) (Director General, COAI) and Dr. Makrand G. Joshi (Director, R&DE (Engrs), DRDO).

Technical co-sponsor: IEEE Consumer Technology Society, Pune Section. Collaborators: the AIT IEEE Student Branch and the AIT ACM Student Chapter.

Source: ICNDIA-2026 brochure; AIT Pune official posts, September 2026.

Since 2018–19 · Faculty In-Charge

OSS Club and Innerve

She looks after AIT's Open Source Software Club and its hackathon, Innerve.

9,500+students, Innerve 9.0

AIT's records list her as the club's staff in-charge from 2018–19. Today she is Faculty In-Charge, with Prof. Kuldeep Hule as co-in-charge. The club started in 2014. Its motto is "May The Source Be With You!"

Besides Innerve, the club runs Anubhav (interview experiences), front-end classes and GRAPHICA, a hackathon for first-year students.

Innerve, "Nerve to Innovate", has run since 2016. Registration is free.

  • Innerve 7, 2023. She was a judge.
  • Innerve 9.0, 7–8 February 2025. 31 finalist teams, a 24-hour finale, 10 tracks. The press named her and Prof. Kuldeep Hule as the faculty behind it.
  • Innerve X, finale 30–31 January 2026. 10,000+ registrations, 45 finalist teams, Major League Hacking (MLH) as event partner. Run under the guidance of the OSS Club faculty.

Sources: Punekar News, February 2025; Free Press Journal and Sahyadri Startups, January 2026; Innerve on Devpost and Devfolio.
